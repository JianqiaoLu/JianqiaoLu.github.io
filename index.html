Current research

Architecture and scaling

I study which architectures and training objectives improve learning at matched compute, and whether those gains persist as models scale.

Long-context efficiency

I explore how sparse attention, memory compression, and reuse across layers can lower long-context costs while preserving the information models need.

Models and systems

I study when computational savings translate into higher throughput and lower latency, while preserving consistent model behavior across training and inference.
